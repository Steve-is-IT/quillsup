Two ready-made calendars for each grade band: a 12-week plan (one lesson a week) and a 36-week plan (a lesson about every three weeks, with review weeks). Both give the counselor three weeks' warning and families two weeks' notice for sensitive lessons. Adjust for your calendar, testing windows and breaks. [COUNSEL TO CONFIRM your notice period]

How the lessons run#

  • 10 core lessons per band, 15 minutes each: Hook 2, Activity 8, Discuss 3, Takeaway and exit ticket 2.
  • Sensitive lessons (see the Counselor Coordination Protocol): K-2 L6 and L9; 3-5 L1 and L6; 6-8 L1, L6, L7, L8; 9-12 L5, L7, L8.
  • Plus lessons for grades 6 to 12 are optional extensions: 6-8 P1 to P3, 9-12 P1 to P4. P1 (trafficking) and P2 (mental health) wait until your specialist or clinician review is done. [VERIFY with the kit's Plus changelog]
  • The optional pre and post surveys take 10 to 15 minutes each (K-2 about 10, oral). Use only what counsel clears. [COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]

Placement rules for sensitive lessons#

  1. Schedule mid-morning or earlier. Avoid the last period and the day before a break.
  2. Avoid Fridays when the counselor is part-time on Fridays.
  3. Allow at least two class meetings after a sensitive lesson before the next one in the same class.
  4. Keep the counselor's free time on the calendar before you commit the date.
  5. Do not teach one on a testing day.

The 12-week plan (one lesson a week)#

Weeks -3 to 0 are preparation. Weeks 1 to 12 are teaching weeks. "Notice" means the family pre-notice goes home; "Heads-up" means the teacher messages the counselor.

Week Activity (all bands)
-3 to 0 (preparation) Week -3: staff session and counselor confirmation for any sensitive lesson in Weeks 1 to 3. Week -1: family letter 1 goes home, with the pre-notice for a sensitive Lesson 1. Week 0: teacher heads-up messages
1 Pre survey (if used). Lesson 1
2 to 10 Lessons 2 to 10
11 Catch-up and review. Post survey (if used)
12 Family showcase or Hackles Day. Counts to the tracker. Teacher debrief

The 36-week plan (a lesson about every three weeks)#

This plan spaces lessons so that skills come back. Teachers revisit Hackles up, Pause, Check, Tell and the Help Ladder briefly in the review weeks.

Fitting the plan to your setting#

Fitting the plan to your setting
Setting Suggestion
Elementary classroom Use the 12-week plan during morning meeting or a rotating specials slot
Middle school advisory The 12-week plan fits an advisory block. Rotate which adult teaches so that each lesson has a counselor available
High school Use health, ELA, CS or an advisory block. Choose the 36-week plan if the school cannot give 12 straight weeks
School library Run each lesson as a 15-minute read-aloud or station within library time, with the classroom teacher present
After-school or youth program Use the 12-week plan with the same adult each week. The program names who plays the counselor role
College orientation or first-year course Use the 9-12 lessons selectively, with a campus counseling referral list. Do not reuse minors' materials without review. [VERIFY with your campus]
Summer program Compress to two lessons a week for five weeks. Keep notice and counselor timing

Common problems#

Common problems
Problem Fix
A holiday knocks out a lesson week Move the whole sequence one week. Do not skip a sensitive lesson's notice date
Counselor is out on the planned day Move the lesson. Never run a sensitive lesson without backup
A disclosure happens in week 5 Follow the flowchart. Pause the next sensitive lesson if the counselor needs the time
Families ask for earlier notice Send it. A longer notice period costs nothing
A class falls behind Drop the optional hook of a non-sensitive lesson before cutting an activity. Never cut the Tell step
