1. What Hackles is#

Hackles teaches children one habit. When something online feels off, they notice the feeling (Hackles up?), stop (Pause), take a second look (Check) and tell a trusted adult (Tell). A false alarm is fine. If something already went wrong, it was never the child's fault.

The kit has 47 lessons of 15 minutes each: ten per grade band (K-2, 3-5, 6-8, 9-12) plus seven Plus lessons for older students (6-8 P1 to P3, 9-12 P1 to P4) that cover topics some states require. Around the lessons you will find slides, one-page lesson cards, six offline classroom tools, training for teachers, materials for families, administrators, colleges and out-of-school clubs, and a searchable web edition.

Terms. The Help Ladder is the tool for "who do I tell, and what if they do not help?" (K-2 uses a picture version, "My Tell-Someone Team"). Hackles Day is the school event. STOP · SAVE · TELL · REPORT is the response drill for threats and image abuse in grades 6-12. SAVE means keep the words, usernames and links. Never screenshot, save or forward an intimate image of anyone under 18.

4. How to use the kit#

  1. Read the Teacher Primer (about 10 minutes).
  2. Read the Disclosure and Incident Guide, and check your school's mandatory-reporting policy and your state's law.
  3. Pick your band. Read the lesson card for Lesson 1 and, if you want more, the Teacher Guide's opening pages.
  4. Tell your counselor before any sensitive lesson (body safety, sextortion, grooming, and the Plus lessons on trafficking and on mental health), and send the family letter. Do not teach Plus Lesson P1 (trafficking) until a trafficking-prevention specialist has reviewed it, or Plus Lesson P2 (mental health) until a counselor or clinician has.
  5. Teach one lesson a week: hook, activity, discussion, takeaway. Print the reproducibles you need.
  6. If you want to measure, run the paper pre/post and keep tallies to class counts, with no names. Check consent rules first.
  7. Send home the family card and reply slip.
  8. Celebrate with a Hackles Day if you like.
  9. Tell us what to fix. [CONTACT: to be added]

7. Release gates (summary)#

Before wide release: a school counselor reviews the sensitive lessons; a child-safety organization or clinician reviews them again; an attorney reviews the license, terms, privacy, trademark and liability; someone checks every hotline, URL, statistic and state-law statement against primary sources; and a small pilot (three to five classrooms per band) tests timing, fun and comprehension.

8. Rebuilding the files#

The Markdown files are the source of truth. The Word and PDF files are generated from them. The Reader, Slides and Lab are generated too. Instructions are in the project's top-level README.txt.
