Week 1: Why This Matters: The Risks, the Evidence and the Hackles Idea#

Academy module: M1. Care level: standard. Slide deck: Week_01_Why_This_Matters.pptx.

Outcomes#

  • CLO 1: Describe the main online risks facing K-12 learners in calm, accurate language, and judge a statistic or claim by tracing it to its primary source and saying what it can and cannot show.
  • CLO 10: Reflect on your own practice, wellbeing and growth edges, and set a 30-60-90 plan.

Required reading (before class)#

  • Hackles Teacher Academy, Module 1: Why This Matters (all parts)
  • Hackles Teacher Academy, Overview and Learning Path (the six competencies A to F)
  • NCMEC, 'NCMEC releases new sextortion data' (2025 counts), ncmec.org/blog/2026/ncmec-releases-new-sextortion-data-2025 [VERIFIED 2026-10-05 in kit]

Optional reading#

  • Finkelhor, Walsh, Jones, Mitchell and Collier (2021), 'Youth Internet Safety Education: Aligning Programs With the Evidence Base,' Trauma, Violence, & Abuse [VERIFY: confirm citation and read the full text]
  • Thorn, 'The State of Sextortion in 2025,' thorn.org [VERIFIED 2026-10-05 in kit]
  • Pew Research Center, 'Teens, Social Media and AI Chatbots 2025' (9 Dec 2025) [VERIFIED 2026-10-05 in kit]

Minute-by-minute agenda (180 minutes)#

Minute-by-minute agenda (180 minutes)
Clock Min Segment What happens
0:00 10 Opening Check-in and group agreements (respect each other; share only what you choose; what happens in role-plays stays in role-plays, but you can always tell a trusted adult). Pass card is always available. Say the routine together: Hackles up? Pause. Check. Tell.
0:10 15 Pre-Course Confidence Survey Academy_Assessments, Section 2. Done privately on paper or on your own device and kept by you; the instructor records completion only. Used again in Week 13.
0:25 20 Gut-Check Line-Up Facilitator Kit method (Half-Day Guide, Segment 1): five statements about teaching online safety, learners line up from 'not at all' to 'completely'. Notice where the room stands. Debrief: what surprised you?
0:45 20 Activity 1.1 Honesty Map, then the seven risk families Academy Activity 1.1 (5 min, private, fold and keep for Week 13). Then a guided tour of the seven risk families and the four features that connect them: pressure, secrecy, a request, a move somewhere more private. Try This Now from Module 1 (3 min).
1:05 10 Break Stretch, water, step outside. Nobody has to explain a longer break.
1:15 25 Activity 1.2 Number Detective Pairs trace one statistic to the organization's own page. Answer: who published it, what year, who was counted or surveyed, reports or survey share? Write one calm sentence for a parent. Concepts Lab Demo 1 Try This Now (dice under a cup, 60 sec) closes the activity as a reminder that a small number can hide a large idea.
1:40 25 Activity 1.3 Warning vs Skill: the Rewrite Game Rewrite fear messages as skill messages. Check: a good skill message names a signal, one concrete action, and a hopeful, shame-free tone.
2:05 30 Scenario Studio 1.A: 'My principal wants an assembly' Role-play the conversation with the principal in trios (principal, teacher, observer). Use both/and: keep the visible kickoff, add ten short lessons. Debrief against the evidence table (active, repeated, skill-based versus one-off assemblies).
2:35 15 Course map and field expectations Walk the syllabus: assignments, the Response Readiness gate in Week 9, background check and placement paperwork timeline, the counselor partner, the AI use policy.
2:50 10 Closing Exit ticket (one sentence: what I will try this week), the open door ('If anything today made you think of something, talk to me or the counselor partner; you are never in trouble for telling'), and a calm breath.

Concepts Lab and Facilitator Kit#

  • Concepts Lab: Concepts Lab Demo 1 Try This Now only (full Demo 1 is Week 2).
  • Facilitator Kit: Gut-Check Line-Up (Half-Day Workshop Guide, Segment 1).

Due, assigned and field#

  • Due this week: Start background-check and placement paperwork. Complete the Pre-Course Confidence Survey. Module 1 self-check is due before Week 2 (mastery is 8 of 10; retakes allowed).
  • Assigned: Reflective Journal entry 1 and Module 1 self-check (both due before Week 2).

Due, assigned and field#

  • Due this week: Capstone plan and materials. Journal entry 14.
  • Assigned: Capstone final package (delivery evidence, evaluation tally, observer form, reflection 500 words) due at the final exam slot or the last day of the term [INSTITUTION].
  • Field tie-in: Field log signed by the mentor. Return any borrowed materials. No student information leaves the school.

Discussion and journal prompts#

  • Discussion: Post one sentence for next year's candidates: what you wish you had known on day one.
  • Journal: Final reflection (500 words, in the capstone package): what changed in how you think about teaching digital safety? What is your next step? Which competency (A to F) is still your growth edge?

Instructor notes#

Celebrate honestly: do not describe the course or the kit as effective or approved. Close the term with the data-handling steps: delete recordings, destroy any paper forms, and remind candidates not to keep student information anywhere.
