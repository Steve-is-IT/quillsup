Nebraska#

Summary: Mandate: Policy-only | Grades: unspecified (K-12 policy; CS instruction K-12 from 2025-26) | Citation: Neb. Rev. Stat. 79-2,137; 79-2,144; 79-3304 | Confidence: Medium

(a) Instruction/policy mandates:

  • Bullying prevention and education POLICY required of each district (Neb. Rev. Stat. 79-2,137, Laws 2008 LB205); electronic abuse is within the bullying definition. No specific instruction/curriculum prescribed. [VERIFIED 2026-10-05: https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=79-2%2C137]
  • School Security Director (79-2,144, last amended Laws 2023 LB705) is to RECOMMEND materials on cyberbullying and digital citizenship; recommendation only, not a student-instruction mandate. [VERIFIED 2026-10-05: https://www.nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=79-2%2C144]
  • Dating-violence education (79-2,142, Laws 2009 LB63) requires age-appropriate dating-violence education; dating-violence policy (79-2,141). Not internet-specific. [VERIFIED 2026-10-05: https://www.nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=79-2%2C142 and ...statute=79-2%2C141]
  • Child sexual abuse (Erin's-law type): No state mandate found in primary sources searched. A search summary indicates bills (LB143 / LB303) that would let schools ADOPT a policy ("should be considered for adoption"); LB143 reported indefinitely postponed; enactment not confirmed. [VERIFY]
  • Computer science (79-3304, Laws 2022 LB1112, amended 2023 LB705): districts must include CS aligned to state standards K-12 from 2025-26; 5 HS credit hours in CS and technology from 2027-28. Digital citizenship is within the CS and technology definition per the standards doc (cites 79-3303). [VERIFIED 2026-10-05: https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=79-3304]
  • 79-2,145 only permits State Board school-security rules (permissive, not instruction). [VERIFIED 2026-10-05: https://www.nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=79-2%2C145]
  • Human trafficking, social media, media literacy instruction: No state mandate found in primary sources searched.

(b) Standards Hackles supports: Nebraska Computer Science and Technology Standards (SBE approved 2024-02-02; the document read is a "Final Draft", high school only): CS.HS.2 (digital citizenship; CS.HS.2.b validity/accuracy of information, CS.HS.2.e safety/security and digital footprints), CS.HS.4.b (protect personal devices/information). K-8 codes not read. [VERIFIED 2026-10-05: https://www.education.ne.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/File_-Computer-Science-and-Technology-Standards_Final.pdf] No health standards found on the NDE Content Area Standards page. [VERIFIED 2026-10-05: https://www.education.ne.gov/contentareastandards/]

(c) Mandated reporting pointer: https://dhhs.ne.gov/Pages/Child-Abuse.aspx: hotline (800) 652-1999 [VERIFIED 2026-10-05: same URL]

(d) Student data privacy: Not researched for Nebraska; Hackles collects no student data. [VERIFY]

(e) Adoption note: Not confirmed. [VERIFY]

Nevada#

Summary: Mandate: Yes (bullying/cyberbullying Week of Respect; personal safety in health course) | Grades: unspecified (personal safety K-12 via health course; Week of Respect all schools) | Citation: NRS 388.1395; NRS 389.031, 389.064 | Confidence: Medium

(a) Instruction/policy mandates:

  • NRS 388.1395 (Week of Respect): each school's governing body delivers information to pupils during the first week of October on preventing, identifying and reporting bullying and cyber-bullying and improving the school environment. Instruction-type mandate, grades unspecified. [VERIFIED 2026-10-05: https://doe.nv.gov/offices/osrle/school-climate/week-of-respect (NDE page quoting NRS 388.1395; the leg.state.nv.us fetch did not return the statute text itself)]
  • NRS 388.139 requires anti-bullying/cyber-bullying language in rules of behavior; NRS 388.133 requires an NDE policy; 388.1342 staff training (policy/training, not student instruction). [VERIFIED 2026-10-05 (headings only): https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-388.html]
  • NRS 389.031/389.064: age-appropriate personal-safety curriculum standards K-12 (hazards incl. unsafe persons, abuse); districts/charter schools must implement within a course of study in health; schools set grade levels and content; parental opt-out. Internet safety not named in the text as read. [VERIFIED 2026-10-05: https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-389.html]
  • NRS 389.072: every pupil receives State Board-approved computer education and technology instruction before grade 6; high school CS regulations may include cyber security. [VERIFIED 2026-10-05: same URL]
  • Human trafficking and social media instruction: No state mandate found in primary sources searched (neither term appears in the NRS 388 text read; none seen in NRS 389).

(b) Standards Hackles supports: 2020 NVACS for Health (adopted Oct 2020; full implementation 2022-23); benchmark codes not read.
