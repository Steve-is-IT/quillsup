Recovery Map Card#

Draw your accounts as boxes. Do not write real account names or passwords. Draw arrows from each account to the thing that can reset it (email, phone number, backup code, another account).

Recovery Map Card
Step What to do
1 Draw 5-8 boxes for the kinds of accounts you have (email, social, game, school, shopping, cloud storage).
2 Draw an arrow from each account to what can reset it.
3 Circle the box with the most arrows pointing out of it: the master key.
4 Star it. This account needs your longest passphrase and the strongest second door.
5 Write the one thing you will do this week to protect it.

Rank the Doors (board or handout)#

  • (a) Password only
  • (b) Password plus a code sent by text
  • (c) Password plus a code from an authenticator app
  • (d) A passkey or a physical security key
  • (e) A password you also use on three other sites, plus a code by text

R3. HOOKS Card and Case Cards C1-C8 (Lesson 3)#

HOOKS Card (one per student)#

HOOKS Card (one per student)
Letter Hook What it sounds like
H Hurry "Within 30 minutes." "Only the first 50." "Right now."
O Official "Security team." "Your friend." "School IT." "Your bank."
O Offer or outrage "You won!" "Free currency." Or "Your account will be closed."
K Keep it quiet "Do not tell your parents." "Do not tell anyone."
S Strange method Gift cards, crypto, reading out a code, moving to another app, paying a stranger you cannot verify

Break the hook: Pause, then Check in a channel you chose, then Tell someone.

Case Cards (print and cut; each case is fictional and generic)#

Case Cards (print and cut; each case is fictional and generic)
Card Case
C1. Account Locked A text says: "SECURITY NOTICE: Your account will be disabled in 30 minutes due to unusual activity. Verify at this link. Reply with the 6-digit code we send to keep your account."
C2. Friend Needs a Code A message comes from a friend's account: "A code got sent to your phone by mistake. Can you read it to me? I will explain later. Please, I am locked out."

C6. Ticket Deal A seller says: "Concert tickets, half price, sold out everywhere. Pay first with the no-protection payment option and I will send them after. Others are waiting, decide in 10 minutes."
C7. Free Currency Giveaway A message from a member of your gaming server says: "Giveaway! Log in with your account at this link to claim 5,000 free currency. Only the first 50 people."
C8. School IT Notice An email "from the school help desk" says: "Your password expires today. Click here to keep your access and confirm your current password. Failure to act will lock you out of grades."
