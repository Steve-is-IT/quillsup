Trust your hackles. Pause · Check · Tell

A free kit by Competence Collective · competencecollective.org · quillsup.org

Out-of-School Edition. Printables for all three age tracks. Every message, link, name and game in these cards is invented. Links end in .example, which is a pretend ending. No child data is collected, ever.

How to use these cards#

Print each set on plain paper or index cards. Cut the cards apart. The leader keeps the "Back" text and reads it aloud after each round. If you only have one printer and ten minutes, print the leader pages (fronts and backs together) and read from the page. Nobody needs to see a card to play.

Three answers for every card. Players answer with their hands, so no answer cards are needed:

How to use these cards
Answer Hand signal Meaning
FISHY Brush a hand up the back of the neck (Hackles Up) "My hackles are up. I think this is a trick."
REAL Star hands (jazz hands) "This looks safe. I know who it is from and I expected it."
PAUSE AND ASK Flat palm like a stop sign (Pause Palm) "I can't tell. I will check a different way or ask a trusted adult."

Two promises, said before every game: "A false alarm is fine. It is never your fault."

Never: make a child read aloud, ask a child to play the trickster, use a real message from your group, or end with "you lost". Pass is always allowed.

These sets leave out on purpose any card about strangers asking where a child lives, asking a child to move to a private chat, or asking for pictures. Those topics belong with a school counselor or a family, not a club (see Start Here, Section 7). If a child brings one up, thank them and use the "If a child tells you something" card.

Local swaps. A few cards mention gift cards, dollars and US-style phone numbers. Swap in your own local equivalents before printing.

Source. These cards are adapted from the Hackles Games and Fun Pack (Spot the Fishy). The Pack's card numbers appear in square brackets so you can look up the full deck.

Set A: Little Hackles (ages 5-7), 18 cards#

Read each card in a silly announcer voice. Read it twice, slowly. After the answer, everyone does the Hackles move for the right action. End every card with: "Hackles up? Pause. Check. Tell."

Set A: Little Hackles (ages 5-7), 18 cards
# Front (read aloud) Answer Back (what the leader says)
A1 A pop-up on a game says: "YOU WON A PONY! TAP NOW! Only 5 seconds left!" [K1] FISHY Hurry plus too good to be true. Pause. Do not tap. Tell a grown-up.
A2 A game chat message says: "Free 1,000 gems! Tell me your password so I can put them in your account." [K2] FISHY Asking for a password is a trick. Passwords are only for you and a trusted grown-up.
A3 A video says: "Don't tell your grown-up. This prize is our secret!" [K3] FISHY A real prize never needs a secret. Pause and tell.
A4 A pop-up says: "Your tablet has a problem! Tap here to fix it right now!" [K4] FISHY Tablets do not talk like that. Pause and find a grown-up.
A5 A pop-up shows a shiny gold gift box and says: "TAP THE BOX FOR YOUR PRIZE!" [K13] FISHY That is the Too-Good Box, Gifty! Wave hello and do not tap.
A6 A pop-up flashes: "ONLY 10 SECONDS LEFT TO BE A WINNER!" [K22] FISHY Real prizes do not count down. Pause.
A7 Your teacher says: "Story time is at 2. Bring your blue book bag." [K23] REAL Known person, normal ask, no secrets. Hackles down.
A8 Grandma sends a photo of her cat in a hat through the family chat your grown-up set up. [K24] REAL Known sender, set up by a grown-up, fun.
A9 After your grown-up installed it, a game says: "Update ready: new hats!" [K27] REAL A grown-up set it up and it asks for nothing.
A10 The family tablet says: "Time's up! Screen break time." [K33] REAL A timer your grown-up set. Ember says: stretch!
A11 A math game your teacher gave says: "Nice work! You finished level 2." [K38] REAL Chosen by a trusted grown-up. Real.
A12 The class pet cam says: "Hamster Hank is awake!" [K44] REAL Silly and real.
A13 A video ad says: "Try our new free game!" [K47] PAUSE AND ASK An ad is not from your grown-up. Pause and ask.
A14 A pop-up asks: "Allow this app to use your camera?" [K49] PAUSE AND ASK A big ask. A grown-up decides.
A15 A picture shows a giant cat taller than a building. It looks real. [K51] PAUSE AND ASK Pictures can be made up. Ask a grown-up, "Is this real?"
A16 A new game says: "Pick a name to play!" [K53] PAUSE AND ASK Ask a grown-up. A good game name is silly and is not your real name.
A17 A grown-up wants to post a photo of you and your friends. [K62] PAUSE AND ASK Pip says: ask, "Do all my friends say OK?" Share what everyone is happy to share.
A18 The tablet says: "Install update now?" [K64] PAUSE AND ASK A grown-up decides.

Quick Catch (8 cards, 10 minutes): A1, A3, A5, A7, A8, A10, A14, A15.

Set A: Trail Walk scenes (10)#

Read the scene. Everyone answers with the hand signal for A, B or C at the same moment. The walker always takes 3 steps. The leader never says "wrong".

Set A: Trail Walk scenes (10)
# Scene A B C Best
TA1 A game says: "Tap to win a spaceship!" Tap it Pause Palm Tell a grown-up Pause, then tell (B and C together)
TA2 A game wants your name and birthday. Type them Pause, ask a grown-up Close your eyes Pause, ask a grown-up (B)
TA3 Your friend says, "Give me your password, I'll be nice." Say yes Say "No thanks, it is only for me and my grown-up" Hide B
TA4 You tapped something by mistake and the screen looks odd. Hide it Tell a grown-up: "I made an oops" Close your eyes B
TA5 Your grown-up says you can play for 20 minutes. Hackles Up Star hands (real) Pause Palm B
TA6 A silly box says: "Free hat made of cheese!" Tap it Wave and say "No thanks, Gifty!" Run away B
TA7 Your friend asks to post a photo of you. Ask first Say nothing Say yes A (ask, "do all my friends say OK?")
TA8 A pop-up asks to use the camera. Tap yes Pause and ask a grown-up Ignore it forever B
TA9 Your friend's first try did not help (they said "later"). Give up Next rung: tell another grown-up Cry B (the Next-Rung Rule)
TA10 A video says: "Don't tell your grown-up!" Keep the secret Pause and tell Share it B

Gifty is always an adult voice. Gifty is a cartoon gift box with a hook on the bow. Gifty is silly, never scary, and never wins.

Set B: Hands-On Hackers (ages 8-11), 24 cards#

Teams of 3 to 5 whisper-think for 10 seconds, then everyone shows an answer on "Peek!". Bonus: a team that names a SPOT clue earns a Pond circle for the whole class. SPOT = Secrets, Pressure, Offers too good to be true, Tricks to move you (a link, a login ask).

Set B: Hands-On Hackers (ages 8-11), 24 cards
# Front (read aloud) Answer Back

B18 A friend at school sends a link with no words: "Click!" [K48] PAUSE AND ASK Ask the friend in person. Their account might have been taken over.
B19 A message says: "I'm from your school. What's your teacher's name?" [K50] PAUSE AND ASK Your school already knows. Ask a grown-up.
B20 A website asks: "Enter your name and age to continue." [K52] PAUSE AND ASK A grown-up decides if that is OK.
B21 A video voice says: "Subscribe for a surprise!" [K54] PAUSE AND ASK A surprise might be fine or fishy. Ask first.
B22 An older cousin says: "Use my account to play, here's my password!" [K55] PAUSE AND ASK Ask your grown-up. Passwords are not for passing around.
B23 A message has only a link and comes from a name nobody knows. [K60] PAUSE AND ASK Do not tap. Ask a grown-up.
B24 A friend says: "Let's swap passwords to be best friends!" [K63] PAUSE AND ASK Best friends do not need passwords. Tell a grown-up if the friend pushes.

Quick Catch (8 cards): B1, B3, B8, B10, B13, B17, B20, B23.

Pause needs a Check. A team that shows PAUSE AND ASK on a clearly FISHY or REAL card earns the bonus circle only by saying how it would check ("I would call the number I already have", "I would ask a trusted adult").

Set B: Club Trail scenes (10)#

Use the Mini Trail in Session 1. Read the scene, give three choices, teams vote with a hand signal. Best = 5 steps, okay = 4, oops-path = 3 and a Fixit card (never a penalty).

Set B: Club Trail scenes (10)
# Scene Best answer
TB1 A pop-up says "Click to win!" Your friend says "Ignore it." Tell a trusted adult too: "I need help with something online."
TB2 A message asks for your game password to "level you up". "No thanks. My password is only for me and my grown-up."
TB3 A link looks almost right: pixe1pals.example. Do not click. The number 1 is standing in for a letter. Ask a grown-up.
TB4 You clicked something and your account is acting odd. Pause, breathe, tell a trusted adult: "I made an oops."
TB5 Someone in the class chat posted a mean joke about a classmate. Do not forward it. Tell a trusted adult. Be kind to the classmate in person.
TB6 A teacher you told says, "I'll look later." Next rung: tell another trusted adult.
TB7 A "survey" wants your school and birthday. Pause. A grown-up decides what to share.
TB8 A video says: "Don't tell your grown-ups, they ruin it." Pause and tell. Fun things can be shared.
TB9 Your cousin on the phone says to pick a family code word. Great idea. Pick a silly one and keep it in the family.
TB10 The tablet says your screen time is up. Take a break. Stretch with Ember.

Set C: Case Files (ages 12-15), 24 cards#

Teams whisper, then everyone shows an answer. The FLAGS checklist helps: Fast (rush or threat), Login ask (password, code, account info), Amazing offer, Gotta keep it secret, Strange sender or link. Every REAL or PAUSE answer needs a second channel: a different way to confirm (open the official app yourself, call a number you already have, ask in person).

All links and numbers are pretend. Swap currency and phone formats for your region.

The Hackles Routine card#

The Hackles Routine card
Step Say Hand signal
Hackles up? "Hackles up?" Hand brushed up the back of the neck
Pause "Pause." Flat palm, stop sign
Check "Check." Fingers to eyes (5-7) or magnifier circles (8 and up)
Tell "Tell." Hand on heart, then reach out

"A false alarm is fine. It is never your fault."

The Help Ladder card#

  1. A friend you trust. "Will you come with me while I tell someone?"
  2. A trusted adult. "I need help with something online. I'm not in trouble. I just need to tell you something."
  3. A report tool or a helpline. "I'm using the report button." (An adult helps.)

Next-Rung Rule: If the first person does not help, you are not done. Tell the next person.

The Good Grown-up Reply#

"Thank you for telling me. It is not your fault. Let's figure it out together."

The Oops card (O.O.P.S.)#

  • Okay: breathe in, breathe out.
  • Off: close the page or turn off the screen.
  • People: tell someone you trust.
  • Secure it: change the password, with an adult's help.

Ember's Warm Breath (calm-down)#

Cup your hands as if holding a small flame. Breathe in for 4. Breathe out for 6. Say, "Warm in, soft out." Do it three times.
