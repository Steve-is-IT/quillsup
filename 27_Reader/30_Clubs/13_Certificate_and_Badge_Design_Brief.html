Part 1: The certificates (print as they are)#

Print on colored paper if you have it. Fill in the name by hand. A child may use a made-up Club name. Leave the date blank or write the month.

Certificate A: Little Hackles (ages 5-7)#

[Logo: Hackles] Hackles Club

This certificate says that

is a Little Hackle!

I can do Hackles up, Pause, Check, Tell.
I know a false alarm is fine.
I know it is never my fault.
I know grown-ups I can tell.

Signed by a leader: __ Date: ______

Trust your hackles. Pause · Check · Tell

Certificate B: Hands-On Hackers (ages 8-11)#

[Logo: Hackles] Hackles Club: Hands-On Hackers

This certificate says that

has practiced spotting SPOT clues, building long, silly, secret passphrases, using the Help Ladder and saying "I need help with something online."

A false alarm is welcome. It is never your fault.

Signed by a leader: __ Date: ______

Trust your hackles. Pause · Check · Tell

Certificate C: Case Files (ages 12-15)#

[Logo: Hackles] Hackles Club: Case Files

This certificate says that

has practiced finding FLAGS, naming a second channel, defending an account, and using the Tell-3 script.

Signed by a leader: __ Date: ______

Trust your hackles. Pause · Check · Tell

Certificate D: Family Night#

[Logo: Hackles] Hackles Family Night

A family has practiced Hackles up, Pause, Check, Tell, together.

We agree: when you tell us something, we will say "Thank you for telling me. It is not your fault."

Signed by a leader: __ Date: ______

Certificate E: Helper (for teens and parent volunteers)#

[Logo: Hackles] Hackles Club Helper

Thank you,

for being a safe adult (or a kind older friend) at Hackles Club.

Signed by a leader: __ Date: ______

Certificate rules#

  • Give a certificate to every child who attended, not to a few. No ranking, no "best".
  • Say the child's name warmly. Say, "You are a Little Hackle" or "You did the work."
  • Never write a score, a grade or a comment about behavior.
  • A certificate is not a record. The Club keeps no list of who received one.
  • Alternative text for a screen reader: each certificate has the same text in the document body.

Part 2: The Hackles Club badge, design brief for the illustrator#

Who this is for#

An illustrator who will draw one badge and a small family of related marks for the Hackles Club: Out-of-School Edition. Read the Hackles Art Direction Brief and Character Bible first (the project owner will send them). The colors, character design and line style come from there and win if anything here differs.

What to make#

What to make
# Asset Use Notes
1 Hackles Club badge (the main one) Sticker, paper badge, iron-on patch, sign A round or rounded-square badge, about 2 inches across
2 Level 1 patch: Notice Hackles Patch Level 1 Same family as the badge, one distinct symbol
3 Level 2 patch: Practice Hackles Patch Level 2 Same family, a second symbol
4 Level 3 patch: Teach Hackles Patch Level 3 Same family, a third symbol
5 Passport stamp set (8 small stamps) Hackles Passport One per skill area: Notice, Pause, Check, Tell, Lock It, Share Smart, Kind and Calm, Fix It
6 Certificate border Four certificates (Part 1) A simple border and a corner icon, black and white version too
7 Sticker sheet Take-home Hackle with quills up, Hackles Up hand, Pause Palm, Tell Wave, Latch, Peeper, Pip, Buddy, Ember, Fixit

The badge: the idea#

A friendly round badge that says "this person knows how to notice and tell."

Centre: Hackle (a baby hedgehog with soft pale-yellow, tangerine-tipped quills) with the quills standing up and a tiny sparkle, big kind eyes, rosy cheeks, a smile. Gender-neutral. Not Sonic: no blue, no sneakers, no speed gags, no attitude.
Ring text (top): HACKLES CLUB.
Ring text (bottom): TRUST YOUR HACKLES.
Four small icons around the ring for the four moves: a hand brushing up the back of the neck (Hackles up), a flat palm (Pause), a magnifier (Check), a hand on a heart reaching out (Tell).

Look and feel#

  • Style: warm, flat, friendly, with ink outlines (#2B2D42). Simple shapes. Readable at 1 inch.
  • Palette (from the Art Direction Brief): Sunshine yellow #FFC93C, Tangerine #FF8A3D, Coral #FF6B6B, Sky #5BC0EB, Mint #9BE564, Teal #2EC4B6, Grape #8E6BBF, Cream #FFF8E7, Ink #2B2D42. Ink is for text and outlines only, never a large fill.
  • Border: a simple scalloped or plain circle. Do not copy or imitate the shape, border style, ribbons, sashes, ranks or iconography of any scouting or other youth organisation's awards. If a design reminds you of one, change it.
  • No real-world logos, characters or marks of any other organisation.
  • Text: a rounded sans-serif. Use the typeface in the Hackles design system if it applies. Never a script font that kids cannot read.

The three level patches#

Each level patch has a different shape and a different symbol, so color is never the only clue.

The three level patches
Level Name Shape Symbol Color accent
1 Notice Circle Quills popping up with a sparkle Sunshine yellow
2 Practice Rounded square A pause palm and a magnifier together Sky
3 Teach Hexagon Two hands passing a small flame (Ember) or a heart reaching out Teal

The design should feel like a family. A child who has all three could put them in a row.

Accessibility#

  • Text high-contrast against the background. Test in grayscale.
  • Never rely on color alone. Each level has its own shape and symbol.
  • The symbols must read at 1 inch.
  • For a screen reader, supply a short alt text for each asset (for example: "Hackles Club badge: a baby hedgehog with soft pale-yellow, tangerine-tipped quills popped up, inside a ring that reads Hackles Club").

Deliverables and specs#

Follow the Hackles technical specs for artwork (the project owner will send them).

  • Vector master (SVG) for every asset, outlined text, flat vector.
  • PNG at 300 dpi, sRGB, transparent background, 2000 px wide for the main badge and 1024 x 1024 for each level patch.
  • A black-and-white line version for photocopying.
  • A one-color version (ink only) for stamps.
  • A print sheet: US Letter portrait, 8.5 x 11 in (2550 x 3300 px at 300 dpi) with six badges to cut out.
  • Name files hackles_club_badge.svg, hackles_patch_level1_notice.svg, and so on.
  • Embroidery notes (optional). If an iron-on patch is ever made, supply a simplified version with at most 5 colors and no fine lines under 1 mm [VERIFY with a patch maker].

Not to do#

  • Do not draw Gifty (the Too-Good Box) on any badge. Gifty is a trickster for games, not an award mascot. [ART REQUEST: Gifty art is not yet in kit/09_Art/characters/.]
  • Do not draw Hackle as a dog or as any recognizable species.
  • Do not use the word "scout" or "merit badge" on any asset.
  • Do not use a flag, an eagle, a fleur-de-lis, a trefoil, a clover with four leaves, or any other emblem that other youth organisations use.
  • Do not add a date or a number that could imply a rank.
  • Do not include any child's face or likeness.

Statement on every badge sheet and patch card#

Print this small text on the back of the sticker sheet or the patch card:

Hackles Club patch. Not affiliated with any scouting or other youth organisation. © 2026 Competence Collective. The Hackles name, logos, characters and artwork are not covered by the CC BY-NC license; print and share them unaltered for classroom and family use. [VERIFY with counsel]

Review and rights#

  • Follow the commission and rights terms the project owner provides in writing.
  • Before any manufactured product (iron-on patches, enamel pins, stickers for sale), run a trademark and design check [COUNSEL TO CONFIRM].
  • The badge is free to print for Club use. It is not for sale.
