Hands-On Hackers (the good kind) (ages 8-10)#

Trust your hackles. Pause · Check · Tell

Name: ______ Class: __

Welcome, Hands-On Hacker! A hacker is a clever puzzle solver. The GOOD kind of hacker uses brain power to keep people safe.
Hackles are what rise on the back of your neck when something feels off. That uh-oh feeling is a gut check, and it is a good one to listen to. A false alarm is fine. Nobody is ever mad about a false alarm.
Your super-moves: HACKLES UP? (notice the feeling), then PAUSE. CHECK. TELL.
You are never in trouble for telling. It is never your fault.
Illustration note the squad (Mateo, Amara, Priya, Kenji, Sam) and Hackle (a baby hedgehog with soft pale-yellow, tangerine-tipped quills that stand up when something is off) waving from a clubhouse door.

Meet the squad: Mateo (puzzle lover), Amara (game builder), Priya (question-asker), Kenji (kind and quick to tell), Sam (artist), and Hackle (a cartoon baby hedgehog, not a real chatbot, whose quills stand up at an uh-oh feeling).
