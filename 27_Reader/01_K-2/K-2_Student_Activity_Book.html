Trust your hackles. Pause · Check · Tell

Grades K-2 (ages 5-7)

For the illustrator and the teacher. This book is picture-first. Children are not expected to read. The teacher reads each instruction aloud (see the Teacher Guide). Every page has a small icon strip at the top: a speaker icon (teacher reads this), a crayon icon (color), a pencil icon (draw), a circle icon (circle), and a line icon (match). Words on pages are 1 to 3 words only, in large rounded type. Text in Illustration note ... brackets describes art for the illustrator and does not print. Style: warm, bright, simple, rounded shapes, diverse children, no scary imagery anywhere. Characters: Maya (6, wheelchair, curly hair, two dads), Omar (7, round glasses, puzzle cube), Lily (5, purple hearing aids, loves music), Dev (6, quiet, sketchbook), and Hackle (mascot, they/them: a baby hedgehog with soft pale-yellow, tangerine-tipped quills that stand up when something is off, and big kind eyes; not a dog). Illustration note character lineup spread for the inside cover. All scenarios are pretend. Never use real children's names, faces, or details. Never ask children to write real passwords, addresses, or personal information.

Hackles are the tingly uh-oh feeling. When your hackles go up, you can do four things. A false alarm is okay! And it is never your fault.

Trace each hand and say the word out loud!

LESSON 1: MY TELL-SOMEONE TEAM#

Page 1: My Helper Hand (pencil icon)

  • Trace your hand here. Or use this one.
  • On three fingers, draw a helper who can listen, help, and keep you safe.
  • Fingers 4 and 5: more helpers if you have them!

Helpers at school (circle icon): Circle the helpers who can help you.

Helpers at home (draw icon): Draw one helper at home.

Page 2: Say it out loud!

Say it with your partner: "I need help."

Your partner says: "Thank you for telling me."

Keep telling until someone helps! (match icon)

Color the path. Point to the next helper.

Check yourself: Circle the people who can be helpers. (circle icon)

LESSON 2: SECRET PASSWORDS#

Page 1: A password is a secret key!

Color the key gold. Color the chest.

Who can know my password? (circle icon) Circle the safe picture.

Page 2: Make a silly password! (Pretend!)

  1. Pick a color or feeling. Draw it in box 1.
  2. Pick an animal or thing. Draw it in box 2.
  3. Pick a silly food. Draw it in box 3.

This is a pretend password. Do not use it for real!

Page 3: Passwords are private!

Draw a hand-stop sign in each scene. (pencil icon)

Which are easy to guess? Which are silly-strong? (match icon)

LESSON 3: PAUSE AND ASK#

Page 1: Pop-up!

Draw a Pause Palm on the pop-up. (pencil icon)

Tricky words! Circle the tricky words. (circle icon)

Page 2: Red or green?

Color the circle red if you Pause and ask. Color it green if it is calm. (A grown-up is still near!)

Page 3: Pause and ask!

Number the panels 1, 2, 3, 4. (pencil icon)

Say: "Can you check this?"

LESSON 4: THE OOPS BUTTON#

Page 1: Press the Oops Button!

Color the button red. Color Dev's face. How does he feel? (circle icon)

Page 2: Oops. Tell. Fixed.

Under each panel, trace the word: OOPS. TELL. FIXED. (pencil icon)

What does a helper say? Color the heart bubble. (crayon icon)

Page 3: My body clues

Color the places where your body might say "Pause!" (crayon icon)

My Oops Button card. (Cut out from the Reproducibles page.) Color it red. Keep it in your pocket or folder.

LESSON 5: PRIVATE VS. PUBLIC#

Page 1: Hackle's House

In the window, draw one thing that is okay to share. (pencil icon) Examples: a favorite color, a favorite animal.

Inside the house, draw one private thing. (pencil icon) Examples: a key, a lock.

(Do not draw your real address!)

Page 2: Sort it! (match icon)

Draw a line from each picture to Window or Inside.

The tricky one! The face photo goes in the middle. Draw a Pause Palm next to it. (Ask a grown-up first.)

Page 3: Put a lock on it! (circle icon)

Draw a lock on the private ones.

LESSON 6: ONLINE FRIENDS VS. REAL FRIENDS#

Page 1: Real friends and online friends

Color the friends I see. Draw a question mark on the online player.

Page 2: Surprise or Tell? (match icon)

Draw a line from each picture to the right box.

Page 3: Game chat: Pause!

Draw a hand-stop sign on the chat. Color the heart-and-hand. (crayon icon)

It is never your fault. Trace the words. (pencil icon)

LESSON 7: KIND CLICKS#

Page 1: Kind or unkind?

Color the kind bubbles. Draw a Pause Palm on the unkind ones. (crayon and pencil icons)

Page 2: What do I do?

Follow the good paths with your crayon. Cross out the path we do not choose. (match icon)

Page 3: My kind heart

Draw or write one kind message you could send a friend. (pencil icon)

Color the Kindness Bubble! Add hearts all around it. (crayon icon)

LESSON 8: REAL OR PRETEND?#

Page 1: Real or pretend?

Color the heart if it is real. Color the wand if it is pretend. (circle icon)

Page 2: The mystery voice

Draw a Pause Palm on Omar's hand. (pencil icon)

A computer can make voices and pictures that sound and look real.

When I am not sure, I: (match icon)

Page 3: Our family code word

Ask your family to pick a silly code word. Do not write it here! Keep it in your head and heart. (The Reproducibles page has a card for your family to keep.)

LESSON 9: MY BODY IS MINE#

Teacher note for this page. Use only after teaching Lesson 9 with the Teacher Guide. These pages show no body detail and no harm. Children practice No. Go. Tell.

Page 1: My body is mine!

Color the bubble all the way around the heart. Color the heart your favorite color. (crayon icon) Everything in the bubble belongs to the child.

Page 2: No. Go. Tell. (pencil icon)

Trace the dotted line from No to Go to Tell.

Page 3: My Helper Hand again

Draw your helpers on the fingers. Who could you tell? (pencil icon)

Keep telling until someone helps. (circle icon)

Circle the helper who listens and helps.

It is never your fault. Say it out loud. Color the heart. (crayon icon)

For families (printed at the bottom of this page): Ask your child, "Who is on your Tell-Someone Team?" Tell them: "You can tell me anything, and you will never be in trouble."

LESSON 10: SCREEN BALANCE#

Page 1: How do I feel?

Draw a line from Lily to the face that shows how she feels after a very long time on the tablet. (match icon)

Page 2: My Balance Plate (pencil icon)

Draw something you like in each wedge. Screens are one wedge, not the whole plate!

Page 3: My break menu (circle icon)

Circle three breaks you would pick.

If a screen ever makes me sad, mad, or worried, I:

Color the word TELL. (crayon icon)

MY TELL-SOMEONE TEAM (Pocket Card)#

Cut on the dotted line. Fold on the middle line. It fits in a pocket or a backpack.

Front (inside left):

My Tell-Someone Team

  1. ____ (home)
  2. ____ (school)
  3. ____ (school)
  4. ____ (extra)
  5. ____ (extra)

Back (inside right):

  • I need help.
  • It is never my fault.
  • I will never be in trouble for telling.
  • Keep telling until someone helps.

Outside back: HACKLES UP? PAUSE · CHECK · TELL
