Lesson 5: Private vs. Public. 15 minutes: Hook 2, Activity 8, Discuss 3, Takeaway and exit ticket 2.

Start with the routine: Hackles up? Pause. Check. Tell. Use the hand signal for the first step (hand brushed up the back of the neck).

Hook · 2 minutes

Hook, read aloud (about 2 minutes). Slide shows the main lines; the full script is here.

[Do: Hold up a picture of a house with a big window on the front and a closed door.] Look at Hackle's house. Imagine Hackle puts a big sign in the front window. Everybody walking by can read it. People Hackle knows and people Hackle doesn't know. What if Hackle put a home address on the sign? Or a phone number? Or a picture of a bedroom? Everyone could see! Hackle's spikes would pop right up. Some things are private. They are for inside the house, for Hackle and Hackle's family and helpers.

Plate 4

Hook

But Hackle does put things in the window that are okay: a picture of a favorite flower, a sign that says 'I love tomato soup.' Those are okay to share.

Hook continued.

But Hackle does put things in the window that are okay: a picture of a favorite flower, a sign that says 'I love tomato soup.' Those are okay to share. We call them public. Public means anyone can see. Online, it is like a big window. When we put something online, lots of people may see it. So today we practice sorting: What is okay to share? What do we keep private? And what do we always do first? We Pause and Check with a grown-up.

Step 1 (1 min): Set the rules Point to the hoops. “Public Window is for things that are okay for anyone to see, like favorite colors. Private Inside is for things we keep for us and our grown-ups. When we're not sure, we Pause and Check with a grown-up.”

Step 2 (5 min): Sort the cards Hold up each card (show the picture, say the word). A child volunteer places it in a hoop, and the whole class shows where it goes with their bodies: arms wide like an open window = Public Window; hug yourself and whisper “click!” like a closed door = Private Inside; Pause Palm = “not sure.” Every child moves on every card, so nobody waits for a turn. (No hoops handy? Skip them. The body vote is all you need.) Cards (see Reproducibles): | Card | Where it goes | |---|---| | My favorite color | Public Window (okay with a grown-up's help) | | My favorite animal | Public Window | | A game I like | Public Window | | My full name | Private Inside | | Where I live (my address) | Private Inside | | My school's name | Private Inside | | My phone number | Private Inside | | My password | Private Inside | | A picture of my face | Check first: ask a grown-up (Pause Palm) | | A picture of me in my pajamas or swimsuit | Private Inside | | When my family is away on vacation | Private Inside | | My pet's silly trick | Public Window (okay with a grown-up's help) | For the “Check first” card, place it at the line between the hoops and say: “Some things are in the middle. When we aren't sure, that's the Pause and Check.”

Timer for these steps: 6 min. Press T to start.

Takeaway (2 minutes together with the exit ticket). Do the hand signals as you say each word.

“Today's lesson: Some things are private. When I'm not sure, I Pause and Check with a grown-up before I share.” Do the chant. Then: “Pause before sharing. Check with a grown-up. Tell: 'Can I share this?'”

Hackles line: If a sharing question makes my hackles tingle, I Pause and Check. A false alarm is fine.

Pick ONE boost inside your 15 minutes. The rest are in the Teacher Guide for another day.

Energizer (30 seconds): “Window or Inside Jump.”: Hold up a card. Everyone springs: arms wide like opening a window (public), or hug yourself and say “click!” like closing a door (private). Not sure? Freeze in a Pause Palm in the middle.

Game variant: “Mystery Bag.”: Put sort cards (or objects: a crayon, a toy key, a card with a house number) in a bag. Pull one out slowly with a drumroll: “Window... or Inside?” Children vote with their bodies.

Chant (clap-stomp): “Colors, critters, snacks to share, / name and address stay in there! / Not sure? Pause! I'll check with care!”

Silly voice / puppet moment: Hackle proudly holds up a sign for the window and starts to read it: “My password is...” Children cover their mouths and shout “INSIDE!” Hackle says, “Oops! Thank you for the Pause!” and swaps in a sign that says “I LOVE TOMATO SOUP.” (The silly mistake is pretend, and no real information is ever said aloud.)

Level-Up Challenge: Inside Hunt. With a grown-up, find three private things at home (a house key, a letter with an address, a password card) and whisper “Inside!” Grade 2: tell a partner why the face-photo card goes in the middle (“Check first”). Spiral: “Who on my Helper Hand can I ask before I share?”
