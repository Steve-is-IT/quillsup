Week 15: Capstone Delivery, Showcase and Looking Forward

Academy module: M8. Time: 3 hours (180 minutes).

Learning outcomes this week

  • CLO 7: Partner with families and communities: explain the program plainly, respond to concern with L.E.A.R.N., and plan and deliver a workshop with a counselor partner.
  • CLO 8: Use simple, honest measures (Tell-Readiness, pre/post tallies, rubrics) and report only what the evidence supports, including what is not known about effectiveness.
  • CLO 10: Reflect on your own practice, wellbeing and growth edges, and set a 30-60-90 plan.

Before class (required reading)

  • Your own capstone evaluation tallies and observation notes.

Optional reading

  • Academy Certificate and Badges (what the certificate does and does not mean)

Discussion prompt

Post one sentence for next year's candidates: what you wish you had known on day one.

Journal prompt

Final reflection (500 words, in the capstone package): what changed in how you think about teaching digital safety? What is your next step? Which competency (A to F) is still your growth edge?
