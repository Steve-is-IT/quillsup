Week 13: Measure, Reflect, Grow

Academy module: M8. Time: 3 hours (180 minutes).

Learning outcomes this week

  • CLO 8: Use simple, honest measures (Tell-Readiness, pre/post tallies, rubrics) and report only what the evidence supports, including what is not known about effectiveness.
  • CLO 10: Reflect on your own practice, wellbeing and growth edges, and set a 30-60-90 plan.

Before class (required reading)

  • Hackles Teacher Academy, Module 8, Parts 1 to 6
  • Assessment Pack, Sections 13 and 14 (Honest note on evidence limits; How to run a simple pre/post analysis)
  • Research folder: 'What Is Known and Not Known' evidence brief (this edition)

Optional reading

  • Facilitator Evaluation and Certificates, Section 7 (How to read the results; no student data)
  • Concepts Lab Demo 13 (Incident Response Drill and Hotwash)

In class

MinutesSegmentWhat we do
10OpeningCheck-in and group agreements (respect each other; share only what you choose; what happens in role-plays stays in role-plays, but you can always tell a trusted adult). Pass card is always available. Say the routine together: Hackles up? Pause. Check. Tell.
25What to measure and what not toThree questions: did they learn, would they act, is it safe to tell? Never ask students to disclose experiences. Do not rank children, teachers or classes. Do not report tiny groups (fewer than ten) [VERIFY your district's suppression rule].
35Activity 8.1 Mini-Tally with synthetic class dataUse the synthetic 6-8 class dataset (research folder, code and data). Compute class percentages, percentage-point change, matched pairs up/same/down and the Tell-Readiness indicator by hand or by the provided script. Then write one honest claim and one claim you may not make.
10BreakStretch, water, step outside. Nobody has to explain a longer break.
30What is known and not knownWalk the evidence brief: what the kit can say; what it cannot. No controlled trial of Hackles exists. Disclosures often go up after lessons, and that can be a sign of trust; fewer or more disclosures do not map neatly to less or more harm.
30Concepts Lab Demo 13: Incident Response Drill and HotwashBlameless review. Apply it to a lesson that flopped (Scenario 5.A): detect, contain, recover, learn.
20Activity 8.2 Teach-Back and 8.3 30-60-90 planTeach a colleague the routine and the three promises in three minutes. Draft your 30-60-90 action plan and three if-then obstacle plans.
10Post-Course Confidence SurveyAcademy_Assessments Section 2 again. Compare with Week 1 in private. Overconfidence note: scores may fall in some items because you know what you did not know.
10ClosingExit ticket (one sentence: what I will try this week), the open door ('If anything today made you think of something, talk to me or the counselor partner; you are never in trouble for telling'), and a calm breath.

Discussion prompt

Write one honest claim about your own teaching (real or invented numbers) and one version you must not write. Peer: ask 'What is your evidence?' and help tighten it.

Journal prompt

Look at your Honesty Map from Week 1 in private. Which items moved left? Which did not? What is your first 30-day action?
