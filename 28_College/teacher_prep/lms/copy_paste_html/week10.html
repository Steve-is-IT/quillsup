Week 10: Every Learner: Language, Disability, Trauma-Informed Practice and Low-Device Homes

Academy module: M7. Time: 3 hours (180 minutes).

Learning outcomes this week

  • CLO 6: Adapt lessons and materials for multilingual learners, learners with disabilities, trauma-affected learners and low-device homes, without lowering any safeguard.

Before class (required reading)

  • Hackles Teacher Academy, Module 7, Parts 1 to 5 including Part 4b (Trauma-informed practice)
  • Inclusion and Equity Guide (Teacher Support Pack)

Optional reading

  • Assessment Pack Section 2, accommodations notes [VERIFY IEP and 504 accommodations with your school]
  • SAMHSA, 'SAMHSA's Concept of Trauma and Guidance for a Trauma-Informed Approach' (2014) [VERIFY citation and availability]

In class

MinutesSegmentWhat we do
10OpeningCheck-in and group agreements (respect each other; share only what you choose; what happens in role-plays stays in role-plays, but you can always tell a trusted adult). Pass card is always available. Say the routine together: Hackles up? Pause. Check. Tell.
20Five principles for every lessonMany ways in; no one is singled out; everyone has someone; assume good intentions in families; safety first, always. Try This Now: circle one step in your lesson plan that assumes a device, a quiet home, fluent English, fine-motor skills or a grown-up at home; write one alternative.
30Activity 7.1 Inclusion AuditAudit your micro-teach plan or a Teacher Guide lesson for access barriers. Write at least three real adaptations. Include a seated option, picture support, a non-speaking way to respond.
10BreakStretch, water, step outside. Nobody has to explain a longer break.
25Language: Activity 7.2 Translate 'I Need Help'Teach 'I need help' in every home language in your class. Verify translations with a fluent speaker or the district translation service [VERIFY]. Never use a child as an interpreter for a sensitive conversation.
25Disability and universal designPractical adjustments across disability types; extra safety teaching points; accessibility of materials (alt text, captions, plain language). Hand signals do not depend on English.
30Trauma-informed practice, six practicesPredictability, choice, safety, calm closings, warmth and respect, collaboration. Language that helps and language that harms. Reactions that look like misbehavior may be coping. Candidates: apply the same practices to this course.
20Low-internet and no-device homesUnplugged by default; never assign home tasks that need a device; low-device does not mean low-risk.
10ClosingExit ticket (one sentence: what I will try this week), the open door ('If anything today made you think of something, talk to me or the counselor partner; you are never in trouble for telling'), and a calm breath.
