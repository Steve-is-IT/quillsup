Quiz M7: Every Learner and Every Family (self-check, 10 items)

Mastery is 8 of 10. Answers and rationales are inside each item. Copy these into your LMS quiz tool or use the QTI file.

  1. A child needs to disclose, and a classmate offers to translate. What do you do?

    • A. Ask the child to write in English
    • B. Do not use a classmate or sibling; offer a drawing or note in any language, a trusted bilingual adult or an interpreter service
    • C. Accept the classmate as interpreter
    • D. Delay the conversation
    Show answer

    Answer B. Children should not interpret for sensitive conversations.

  2. Why does the kit say a bilingual buddy should be a choice and rotate?

    • A. So no child becomes the permanent translator or is put on the spot
    • B. Because translation is illegal
    • C. Because buddies are never helpful
    • D. Because it gives extra points
    Show answer

    Answer A. Pairing is a support, not an obligation.

  3. A newcomer to the class cannot read an English poster about 'hackles.' What helps?

    • A. Ask the student to sit out
    • B. Pictures, gestures and the four hand signals, pre-taught key words, and teaching 'I need help' in home languages, verified by a fluent speaker [VERIFY]
    • C. Simplify by removing all safety content
    • D. Assign a longer text
    Show answer

    Answer B. Hand signals do not depend on English.

  4. What does Module 7 say about low-internet and no-device homes?

    • A. Exclude those students from the lesson
    • B. Assign online homework anyway
    • C. Treat low-device homes as a problem to fix
    • D. Do practice in class, never assign tasks needing a device at home, and remember low-device does not mean low-risk
    Show answer

    Answer D. The kit is unplugged by default.

  5. A student laughs and withdraws during a heavy topic. What does the kit suggest?

    • A. Ignore all behavior
    • B. It may be coping; respond with calm and curiosity, then check in privately
    • C. Discipline immediately
    • D. Call the family at once
    Show answer

    Answer B. Reactions that look like misbehavior may be a child coping with a heavy topic.

  6. What does L.E.A.R.N. stand for?

    • A. Look, Explain, Argue, Reply, Notify
    • B. Leave, End, Avoid, Refuse, Never
    • C. Listen, Empathize, Agree where you can, Respond with facts and options, Next step
    • D. Learn, Educate, Assess, Review, Navigate
    Show answer

    Answer C. A five-step response to parent pushback.

  7. A parent says, 'You are putting ideas in their heads.' Which reply fits?

    • A. 'That is a thoughtful worry. The lessons avoid graphic detail and give children tools; they usually meet these things online already' [VERIFY any research cited]
    • B. 'Our principal decided; I cannot discuss it.'
    • C. 'You are wrong, and the statistics prove it.'
    • D. 'Your child will be fine, so do not worry.'
    Show answer

    Answer A. Respond with facts and options, not an argument.

  8. A family member could be involved in a child's disclosure. Which message to the family is appropriate?

    • A. A message copying the child's words
    • B. None until you have followed the Disclosure Guide and your policy; do not use a routine concern message
    • C. A call to the family before reporting
    • D. A warm routine message describing the concern
    Show answer

    Answer B. Sharing news of a concern applies to small, non-urgent matters only.

  9. What does 'everyone has someone' mean in practice?

    • A. Every child must name a parent
    • B. Every child must have a phone
    • C. Make sure every child can name a safe adult, and that school helpers count
    • D. Every adult must be a counselor
    Show answer

    Answer C. Some children have no safe adult at home, so school helpers matter.

  10. In the Inclusion Audit, which step in a lesson plan should be flagged?

    • A. A step with a pass card
    • B. A step that uses a hand signal
    • C. A step with pictures
    • D. A step that assumes a device, a quiet home, fluent English, fine-motor skills or a grown-up at home
    Show answer

    Answer D. Circle it and write one alternative.
