8. Do not cite these (the kit removed or corrected them)

The Round 1 check could not confirm the following, or found them wrong. Do not repeat them in your assignments or your workshop.

  • "Over 80,000 sextortion reports overall" and "a 37 percent rise" attributed to NCMEC (the counts are more than 50,000 in 2025 and more than 36,000 in 2024).
  • "1 in 25 teens" as a Thorn figure (Thorn reported 1 in 17; 1 in 25 is a UNICEF figure for some countries).
  • "38 victims referred for suicide intervention" as a sextortion statistic (it belongs to a cryptocurrency investment fraud section of the IC3 report).
  • The IWF "8,029 images and videos, up 14 percent" framing (the report gives 4,586 AI images and 3,443 AI videos).
  • "AI voice scams up 1,200 percent in 2025" and a vendor's "about 200 phishing pages in a week" claim.
  • "NSA GenCyber camps run every summer" (NSA announced no 2026 program; confirm any future status) and "SANS CyberStart is open" (it has closed).
