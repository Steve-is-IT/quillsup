Week 1: Why This Matters: The Risks, the Evidence and the Hackles Idea

Academy module: M1. Time: 3 hours (180 minutes).

Learning outcomes this week

  • CLO 1: Describe the main online risks facing K-12 learners in calm, accurate language, and judge a statistic or claim by tracing it to its primary source and saying what it can and cannot show.
  • CLO 10: Reflect on your own practice, wellbeing and growth edges, and set a 30-60-90 plan.

Before class (required reading)

  • Hackles Teacher Academy, Module 1: Why This Matters (all parts)
  • Hackles Teacher Academy, Overview and Learning Path (the six competencies A to F)
  • NCMEC, 'NCMEC releases new sextortion data' (2025 counts), ncmec.org/blog/2026/ncmec-releases-new-sextortion-data-2025 [VERIFIED 2026-10-05 in kit]

Field tie-in

Placement forms submitted. In-service teachers: tell your administrator you are taking this course and that you will deliver two supervised lessons.

Discussion prompt

Pick one statistic from this week's readings. Post the link to the organization's own page, what was counted (reports or a survey share), and one calm sentence you could say to a parent. If you could not confirm the figure, say so and mark it [VERIFY].

Journal prompt

What comes to mind when you hear 'online safety': a fear, a rule, a memory, a headline? Which of the seven risk families do you know least about, and what would help?

Reminder: no real student names, work, passwords or incident details appear in any assignment. Hackles up? Pause. Check. Tell.
