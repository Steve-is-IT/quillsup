Quiz M5: Running a 15-Minute Lesson (self-check, 10 items)

Mastery is 8 of 10. Answers and rationales are inside each item. Copy these into your LMS quiz tool or use the QTI file.

  1. Which question is open rather than closed?

    • A. 'Is that right?'
    • B. 'Was that a scam?'
    • C. 'What made you think it was a scam?'
    • D. 'Did you tell an adult?'
    Show answer

    Answer C. Open questions invite explanation.

  2. How long should wait time be after asking a question?

    • A. 30 seconds
    • B. Under one second
    • C. About 3 to 5 seconds
    • D. Two minutes
    Show answer

    Answer C. Wait time raises quality and participation.

  3. You are 12 minutes in and the activity is not finished. What does the time rescue table say?

    • A. Add a second activity
    • B. Skip the takeaway
    • C. Extend the lesson by 10 minutes
    • D. Cut one round of practice; keep the takeaway and the open door
    Show answer

    Answer D. Never cut the takeaway and the open door.

  4. Which is one of the 'four mistakes to avoid above all'?

    • A. Giving students a role
    • B. Promising secrecy
    • C. Starting with a hook
    • D. Using a hand signal
    Show answer

    Answer B. The four: making a child the example, promising secrecy, ignoring the open door, teaching without prep for disclosures.

  5. A student starts a long personal story in front of the class. What do you do?

    • A. Let the story continue so everyone learns
    • B. Tell them to stop and not speak again
    • C. Ask follow-up questions in front of the class
    • D. Thank them, redirect gently ('let's keep the details between us and talk privately after'), and follow up that day
    Show answer

    Answer D. This protects the student's privacy and keeps a path open for disclosure.

  6. Only five students are active during the 8-minute activity. Which rubric row and design rule does this violate?

    • A. Active practice: every student should do the skill at least twice, so redesign the activity
    • B. Skill focus: the skill is unnamed
    • C. Safety and closure: the close was calm
    • D. Tone and language: no shaming occurred
    Show answer

    Answer A. The activity design rule is that everyone does something.

  7. A student's exit ticket says, 'Something happened on my account and I don't want to say.' What is the right step?

    • A. Read exit tickets the same day, treat it as a possible disclosure, follow the First 10 Minutes and your policy, and involve the counselor
    • B. Contact the family first without checking policy
    • C. Post the note on the board to ask who wrote it
    • D. Wait until the end of term to read the exit tickets
    Show answer

    Answer A. Module 5 says to check exit tickets for worrying content on the same day.

  8. What is the 'I do, we do, you do' pattern?

    • A. Students teach, teacher watches, parents grade
    • B. Teacher models, the class tries together, then pairs or groups practice twice
    • C. Teacher talks, class listens, class takes a test
    • D. Teacher reads, students copy, students sign
    Show answer

    Answer B. Typical timing: 1 minute I do, 2 minutes we do, 5 minutes you do.

  9. Which hook follows Module 5's hook rules?

    • A. A three-minute statistics lecture
    • B. 'Imagine something terrible happens to you online.'
    • C. Hold up a fake pop-up card and ask for a thumbs up or down: 'Detectives, is this real?'
    • D. A graphic news story about a victim
    Show answer

    Answer C. A good hook is curious, calm, short and names the skill. It never scares.

  10. The Peer Coaching Guide says not to add up Fun, Safe, Skill-based ratings or compare teachers. Why?

    • A. Because the ratings are always wrong
    • B. Because totals are required by law
    • C. Because teachers should never be observed
    • D. A rating is a conversation starter, not a grade; patterns matter more than single items, and only one grow is given
    Show answer

    Answer D. The guide says to pick one grow and avoid comparing teachers.
