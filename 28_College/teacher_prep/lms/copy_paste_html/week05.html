Week 5: Teaching Safety That Works

Academy module: M4. Time: 3 hours (180 minutes).

Learning outcomes this week

Quiz M3: How Young People Live Online (self-check, 10 items)

Mastery is 8 of 10. Answers and rationales are inside each item. Copy these into your LMS quiz tool or use the QTI file.

  1. Which four patterns does Module 3 teach as showing up in every app?

    • A. Speed, Cost, Volume, Size
    • B. Pressure, Secrecy, The Ask, The Move
    • C. Name, Age, Place, School
    • D. Games, Chats, Video, Feeds
    Show answer

    Answer B. Every risk in Module 1 is a variation on these four.

  2. Which four adult reactions does Module 3 say make children stop telling?

    • A. Panic, punishment, lecture, minimizing
    • B. Delay, distraction, laughter and gifts
    • C. Phone calls, e-mails, texts and letters
    • D. Praise, thanks, questions and silence
    Show answer

    Answer A. Each ends the conversation. Take them off the table in advance with the promise: you will not be in trouble for telling me things.

  3. Which question is the best open question for a student about a game?

    • A. 'Do you play every night?'
    • B. 'Is that game safe?'
    • C. 'What do you like about it, and what is annoying?'
    • D. 'You are not talking to strangers, right?'
    Show answer

    Answer C. Open questions invite explanation. Yes and no questions and loaded questions end conversations.

  4. Which AI companion idea is part of the kit's 'fact sheet, not a scare'?

    • A. It forgets everything you type
    • B. It can agree too much, so it is not a substitute for a trusted adult
    • C. It is a person on the other end
    • D. It is always a reliable source of facts
    Show answer

    Answer B. The kit lists: not a person, can be wrong, can agree too much, remembers and stores, not a substitute for a trusted adult.

  5. What did Pew Research Center report in December 2025 about US teens and AI chatbots?

    • A. 95 percent, about half daily
    • B. 64 percent say they use AI chatbots, about 3 in 10 daily
    • C. 99 percent, all daily
    • D. 14 percent, about 1 in 100 daily
    Show answer

    Answer B. [VERIFIED 2026-10-05 in the kit; 1,458 teens surveyed Sept 25 to Oct 9, 2025.]

  6. A speaker says 'one in three teens discuss serious matters with AI instead of a person.' What is wrong?

    • A. The statistic is correct as stated
    • B. Common Sense reported about one in three AI-companion users, not one in three of all teens
    • C. The true figure is one in three of all adults
    • D. Common Sense reported that nobody does this
    Show answer

    Answer B. The denominator matters. Round 1 corrected the kit on this point.

  7. Why does Module 3 say 'a group chat is not a private room'?

    • A. Anything can be screenshotted, forwarded or shown to others
    • B. Group chats are always public websites
    • C. Group chats delete messages automatically
    • D. Only teachers can see group chats
    Show answer

    Answer A. This is the key idea for children about group chats.

  8. A chat goes quiet whenever one student posts. Which upstander moves does the kit's 6-8 'Group Chat Dynamics' lesson practice?

    • A. Screenshotting and posting the chat publicly
    • B. Waiting to see what happens
    • C. Joining in so you are not the next target
    • D. A quiet check-in, saying 'not cool' in the chat, leaving the chat, and telling a trusted adult
    Show answer

    Answer D. These are the upstander moves named in Module 3.

  9. A teacher tells students that several state attorneys general have 'proved' a game company harms children. How should this be corrected?

    • A. These are allegations and fast-moving developments, not settled findings, so do not state them as fact in class [VERIFY current status]
    • B. Say the actions were all dismissed
    • C. Remove the topic entirely and never discuss games
    • D. Add that the companies have admitted guilt
    Show answer

    Answer A. Module 3 and the evidence file label them allegations.

  10. In Week 4's Curiosity Interview, which behavior fits the five-step script?

    • A. Correcting the partner's app choices
    • B. Listening about 70 percent of the time and reflecting back what you heard without judging
    • C. Writing down names and details to keep
    • D. Offering a lecture on risks
    Show answer

    Answer B. Be curious, listen more than you talk, reflect back, ask open questions, offer do not impose.
