F. Stepped wedge (every class gets the lessons, in steps), m=20, ICC 0.05, two-way fixed effects on class-period means,

Rule of thumb for a small college: a single study of 4 to 9 classes can detect only large effects.
Plan to pool classes across terms or sites, pre-register, and treat the result as a signal.
