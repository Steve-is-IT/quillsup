Grades 6-8Ages 11-13

Case Files

Ten lessons for students aged 11 to 13. Students play analysts. They read a short, fictional case file about a made-up young person, spot the pattern, and practise getting help.

There are no scare statistics and no graphic detail. Students this age respond to respect, clear steps and practice.

This page is for the grown-up. Children do not need to visit this site.

III.A three-step start

Teach it this week

  1. Read two things first

    Read the Teacher Primer (about 10 minutes) and the Disclosure and Incident Guide. Check your school's mandatory-reporting policy.

  2. Get Lesson 1 ready

    Read the card for Lesson 1. Set up the Quiet Corner and print the Pass cards. Tell your counselor now which weeks Lessons 6, 7 and 8 will fall. Send the family letter.

  3. Teach Lesson 1

    Teach Lesson 1, Help Ladder: Real Practice. It takes 15 minutes. Read the exit tickets the same day and follow up privately on any worrying note.

After that, teach one lesson a week for 10 weeks. Each Teacher Guide has its own pacing guide, and that one wins.

V.A note for the adult who teaches

Sensitive lessons and the counselor heads-up

Lessons 6, 7 and 8 ask students to think about abuse and exploitation. They contain no explicit content, no real images, no graphic descriptions and no step-by-step offender tactics. Even so, they need extra care. The Counselor Heads-Up and Opt-Out Toolkit marks these lessons as sensitive.

Lesson 6. Grooming PatternsTrust-building, secrecy, isolation

Plus P2. Scroll ScienceMental health, loneliness, compulsive use; hopelessness card. Counselor or clinician review required before use. Family note; opt-out honored; alternate ("My Limits")

Still pending. The sextortion, grooming and deepfake lessons (Lessons 6 to 8) have not yet had a trauma-informed review by a school counselor and a child-safety expert.

Teachers and counselors still report under their own policy and state law. Hackles does not change that.

Before you teach one of these lessons

  1. Send the family pre-notice at least 5 school days before Lessons 7 and 8, with an easy opt-out.
  2. Give your counselor a heads-up before the lesson, and have the counselor available on the day. The Counselor Heads-Up and Opt-Out Toolkit has the message.
  3. Have the opt-out and alternate-activity plan ready. Each lesson has one inside it.
  4. Teach these mid-week and early or mid-day. Never right before a long break, a test or a major school event, and never Lessons 6, 7 and 8 on consecutive days.
  5. Read the full lesson the day before, disclosure box included, and the Disclosure and Incident Guide.
  6. Hold Plus Lesson P1 until a trafficking-prevention specialist has reviewed it, and Plus Lesson P2 until a counselor or clinician has.

VI.The family piece

For families of ages 11 to 13

A family card and a reply slip come home with each lesson. Families get a pre-notice before the sensitive lessons and can ask to opt out.

Three questions to try at home

  1. What does "Hackles up? Pause. Check. Tell." look like for you in a group chat?
  2. Which of your accounts would be hardest to lose? How do you protect it?
  3. What would make it easier to tell me about something hard online?

These come from the ten conversation starters for ages 11-13 in the Family Engagement Pack. The pack also has the letters, the family code word guide and a page on what to say if your child tells you something scary.

If your child shares a mistake, begin with “Thank you for telling me.”

If a child is in danger nowCall your local emergency number. This site cannot respond to emergencies.

VII.Practice and play

Lab tools and games that fit

All six Lab tools reach this age. They run offline in a browser. They use JavaScript, send nothing anywhere and store nothing.

  • Spot the Fishy K-12Notice the clues that make a message feel fishy, and sort real from fishy.
  • Passphrase Lab Grades 3-12Build practice passphrases and compare how long each would take to guess.
  • Data Detective Grades 3-8See how small clues add up to a made-up person.
  • Secret Code Lab Grades 3-8Scramble and unscramble messages picked from a list. Nothing personal can be typed in.
  • Hackles or Not Grades 2-8Practise the whole routine on short situations: notice, then choose Pause, Check or Tell.
