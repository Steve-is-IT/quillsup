Quills Up

Every child should know what to do when something online feels wrong. One habit covers it: notice the feeling, stop, take a second look, and tell a trusted adult.

Quills Up is building a free cyber safety kit for kindergarten through grade 12: short lessons for every age, materials for families, and training for the adults who teach it. No accounts. No tracking. No student data, ever.

Free for every school, library and family. The kit is in review and will be released here.
