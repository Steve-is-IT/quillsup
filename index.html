The Hackles kit · a field guide to the wild web, for ages 5 to 18

Quills Up

Hackles up? Pause. Check. Tell.

Hackles is a free set of 47 fifteen-minute lessons that help children notice when something online feels wrong and know exactly what to do next.

I.Pick an age range

Which age do you teach or raise?

This site is for the grown-ups who teach and raise children. Each door is a plain link to that age's ten lessons, a three-step start and the downloads that fit. Nobody is asked their age.

  • Grades K-2Ages 5-7

    K-2: Hackles

    Telling a trusted grown-up, and asking before clicking. Secrets and surprises, and my body is mine.

  • Grades 3-5Ages 8-10

    The Help Ladder, passphrases and spotting scams. Game-chat red flags and a family code word.

    Open the 3-5 page

  • Grades 6-8Ages 11-13

    6-8: Case Files

    Account defense, phishing and AI chatbots. Grooming patterns and pressure online, taught calmly.

  • Grades 9-12Ages 14-18

    9-12: Your Digital Defense Plan

    Threat modeling, passkeys and verifying fakes. Helping a friend, and a personal defense plan.

    Open the 9-12 page
